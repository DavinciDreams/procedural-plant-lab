# Procplants PRD implementation status

Updated: 2026-08-10

This is the execution checklist for `new features.md`. It distinguishes features that exist in the standalone generator from features supplied only by the downstream Tellus runtime. Metadata estimates are not counted as real runtime LOD, wind, or export implementations.

## Implemented in Procplants

- Deterministic Weber-Penn, branch-module, herbaceous graph, guided-vine, and space-colonization/swept-tube backends, plus a tested parametric stochastic L-system/turtle foundation.
- Species presets, hybridization, ecology profiles, and golden-angle placement. Added sugar maple, weeping willow, windswept bonsai, deadwood snag, and twilight fantasy tree with low-cost real LOD sets.
- Instanced organ rendering plus bounded reusable prototypes and instanced stems in the lab.
- Branch-relative foliage frames, upward-facing blade normals, folded/venated leaf surfaces, spreading crowns, junction blending, and deterministic tree traits.

## Implemented with explicit scope or heuristic limits

- The public compiler produces separate LOD geometry, the standalone hero switches tiers by camera distance, and Weber-Penn trees, canonical graph-backed woody cohorts, and eleven audited fern/ground-cover cohorts in the dense lab use the placement pool. `ProcPlantRuntimePackage.lods` remains compact budget/distance metadata; other herbaceous cohorts still use authored fallback geometry.
- Space colonization and swept tubes are selected by one opt-in production preset with real constructive lobe and spline crown sculpting plus per-chain tube overrides. Watertight branch unions remain outside the production compiler; collars and chain overlap swell surfaces without establishing manifold boolean topology, and the separate junction-research entry does not alter production output.
- Canonical generator DAGs preserve exact legacy output, while edited supported procplant graphs execute real typed stages with independent seeds, compatible rewires, and validated scopes. Specialized backends and unrestricted new parameter/stage semantics remain fail-closed. Macro-rule defaults are deterministic authoring transforms, not calibrated biological models.
- Spatial force fields now deform public graph branch curves by authored position/radius/direction. This remains bounded endpoint deformation rather than a physical solver, and downstream mesh geometry must be rebuilt from the returned graph.
- The resource simulator distributes vigor and carries reserve/activation/prune evidence across steps from caller-supplied or deterministic crown-occlusion probes. The opt-in topology adapter applies confirmed self-prunes and terminal `branch-tip` dormant-bud shoots, but bud selection and shoot dimensions remain bounded procedural heuristics; the model does not perform spectral/translucent radiative transfer.
- Weber-Penn baked trim operates on stable owner stems and descendant closure, with native-leaf and crown-fill cleanup. Branch-tip anchors and normalized range/height selectors do not own one lossless baked range and remain unsupported.
- Material graphs produce byte-bearing PBR/KTX2 resources, generated templates and every LOD have UV0, mixed templates partition into bark/organ surfaces, and the visual editor, generated live shaders, source-material adoption, grouped export, and imported attachment materialization are wired behind lazy paths. The opt-in injected-device WebGPU compute/indirect runtime exists, but downstream indirect rendering remains unwired.

## Still missing from the PRD

- Edited typed DAG execution for specialized backends and unrestricted new stage semantics or parameters. Supported procplant-graph rewires, scopes, and independent seeds are real rather than projections.
- Weber-Penn baked trim beyond owner-stem/descendant operations: branch-tip, normalized range, and height-plane selectors plus Weber organ placement remain unsupported. Primitive and imported-mesh/BVH guide queries are implemented; vine and space-colonization collision remain static compile-time approximations, and deforming meshes require index rebuilds.
- Production watertight swept-branch unions remain missing beyond the bounded research harness. Its frozen accepted three-way hub is a closed audited two-manifold at 7,388 triangles versus 45 for comparable five-sided collar tubes; a 3-by-3 angle/radius sweep accepts 6 cases and safely rejects 3. Curved or multiple junctions, seam-preserving swept-ring integration, adaptive remeshing, and a cost-competitive LOD strategy remain blockers. Spline crowns, per-chain tube editing, imported-surface collars, and branch/accessory materialization are implemented, but collars do not establish manifold boolean topology.
- Material/export extensions beyond the bounded current contracts. Generated shaders use UV0 and delegate normal expressions to baked/standard normal handling; custom-shader export is absent; imported KTX2 live preview needs a configured renderer decoder; USDA material packages accept PNG rather than KTX2; binary USDC and automatic consumer-side LOD switching remain out of scope.
- Calibrated scientific growth simulation. Deterministic crown-occlusion probes, multi-step resource/light state, activation, growth accumulation, prune hysteresis, and first-class terminal dormant-bud shoot materialization are implemented. Species-calibrated phenology/allometry, spectral/translucent radiative transfer, and physiological validation remain future work.
- Runtime performance proof on target hardware. There is no real WebGPU adapter/device benchmark or downstream indirect-draw adoption, and no representative-device Tellus FPS/capacity claim.

Both Tellus PRs remain open drafts. No served/deployed runtime or representative-device performance claim is made.
